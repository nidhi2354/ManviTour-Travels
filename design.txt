A1. BRAND SOCH (design ka ek line me logic)
---------------------------------------------------------------
    "Kaala = bharosa aur premium.  Golden = energy aur action.
     Safed = saaf jaankari."

    Visiting card kaala hai + logo golden hai -> website bhi wahi
    identity follow karegi. Isliye:

      - KAALE blocks    = Hero, Why Choose Us, CTA ke aas-paas, Footer
      - SAFED blocks    = Services, Packages, How It Works, Contact
      - GOLDEN          = sirf buttons, badges, icons, numbers
                          (accent hai -- background nahi banega)

    RULE: Golden ko background ki tarah sirf 1 baar use karenge
          (CTA Banner). Zyada peela = sasta dikhta hai.

---------------------------------------------------------------
    PRIMARY -- Golden (logo ke suraj/pahiye se)

      Token          Hex        Kahan use hoga

################################################################################
  PART E  --  MOTION, ACCESSIBILITY, ASSETS
################################################################################

E1. MOTION (kam rakhenge -- business site hai, showreel nahi)
    - Transition duration: 200-300ms, ease-out
    - Card hover  : translateY(-4px) + shadow
    - Button hover: translateY(-2px) + colour change
    - Section entry: fade-up 20px, ek baar, stagger 80ms
    - Hero illustration: float animation 6s infinite (already CSS me hai)
    - prefers-reduced-motion: animation OFF kar dena

E2. ACCESSIBILITY
    - Poore page par SIRF EK <h1> (Hero wala)
    - Heading order kabhi skip nahi (h2 ke baad h4 nahi)
    - Har image par asli alt text ("Toyota Innova Crysta 6 seater")
    - Icon-only button par aria-label
    - Form: har input ka <label>, error aria-describedby se juda
    - Focus ring har jagah dikhe (outline: none akela mat karna)
    - Text contrast 4.5:1 minimum -- isiliye brand-700 bana hai
    - Mobile drawer: ESC se band, focus trap, background scroll lock

E3. ASSETS CHAHIYE (client se)
